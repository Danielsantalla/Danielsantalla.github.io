Building the gameplay in Unity

My work on Chaos Unleashed! extended across technical art, animation, VFX and programming. I assembled gameplay objects and prefabs in Unity, connecting the visual side of the game with its behavior.

Heroes, enemies and abilities

I helped build the heroes and their abilities, including attacks, powers and ultimate moves. I animated characters, designed how attacks would work, implemented them in Unity and created their sound effects. I also worked on enemy animation states, including death and freezing. The original character illustrations were not my work.

Connecting systems

I wrote C# code to connect character behavior to CMS services and modified the CMS-side configuration that controlled those characters.

An animation pipeline

I developed an AI-assisted animation workflow using Higgsfield and After Effects. I used specific prompts to direct the character motion, then processed the resulting videos in After Effects to turn them into game-ready animation assets.

About

I’m Daniel Santalla, a Technical & VFX Artist based in Mérida, Mexico.

I work remotely with game teams, creating real-time visual effects, shaders, materials and 2D & 3D animation in Unity. I combine technical art with C# programming, gameplay implementation and art-pipeline development.

My work includes LEGO® Party! and Moving Out 2 at SMG Studio, Party Animals at Recreate Games, and Chaos Unleashed! and Bingo Paradise! at Torpedo Labs.

I developed Art Studio VR and Desktop Fishes independently. I also compose music, create sound effects, and share practical Unity and game-art techniques through Under 60 Sec. I co-founded Cometa Games and have taught Unity and game development at Universidad Modelo.

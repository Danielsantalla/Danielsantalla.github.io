Privacy and YouTube terms

Social statistics and conversations on tutorial pages.

Terms of use for YouTube features

By using this website’s YouTube-powered features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Select “Agree and show YouTube” to accept this privacy policy and those terms before statistics, comments or author images are displayed. This choice applies only to the current page and is not saved in cookies or browser storage. Local tutorial videos and links to YouTube remain available without using these features.

Public comments and statistics

Tutorial pages use the YouTube API to show public video statistics and comments, including author names, profile links, comment text and dates. X statistics and reply references come from the X API. Each platform is shown separately, with links to the original conversation.

Snapshots are refreshed weekly. Data older than 14 days is not displayed. If a refresh or source is unavailable, some statistics or comments may be missing. This website does not collect a social login or offer a comment submission form. To comment, follow the link to the original platform.

Google profile images

After you agree to show YouTube content, author images may load from Google servers. Loading an image sends a request to Google, including your IP address. Images use a no-referrer policy and lazy loading.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Optional X replies

X reply embeds load only when you select “Load X replies.” This contacts X, sends your IP address and allows X’s embedded content to use cookies. You can use the original reply links without loading embeds here. See X’s Privacy Policy.

Questions or removal requests

For questions about this display or to request removal of a cached comment or source, contact Daniel. Changes to an original comment are managed on YouTube or X and may not appear here until a refresh.

The Art Studio VR privacy policy covers that app separately.
